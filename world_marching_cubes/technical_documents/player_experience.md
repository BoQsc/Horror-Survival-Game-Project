# Player Experience: The "Manhattan World" (Octahedron-First)

The transition to an **Octahedron-Centric** world fundamentally changes how the world feels. It moves from an organic "putty" world to a structured "crystalline" world.

## 1. Solidity: "Crystal Clearness"
- **The Feel**: The world feels **Healed and Structured**. Because the Octahedron is the primary shape, the landscape naturally forms sharp 45-degree ramps and ridges.
- **Movement**: Walking and climbing feel perfectly predictable. You're not sliding off "rounded" hills; you're climbing natural, solid geometric slopes that feel like they were chiseled for you.

## 2. Precision: "Hit What You See"
- **The Feel**: Interaction will feel **Sharp and Surgical**. When you hit a spot with a pickaxe or a shovel, the terrain will respond exactly where you clicked.
- **Visual Feedback**: Using Voxel-Perfect targeting means your "Selector Box" will snap to the grid. You’ll know exactly which cube you’re about to remove before you hit it, eliminating the frustration of "missing" a small bit of floating terrain.

## 3. Structural Freedom: "Diamond Framing & Naturalism"
- **The Feel**: Placing terrain with the **Octahedron Tool** will feel like "growing" crystalline structures. You’ll be able to quickly frame out slanted roofs, sharp ridges, and perfect staircases. 
- **The Result**: You get the best of both worlds. You can use cubes for foundations and octahedrons for everything else—slanted peaks, jagged walls, and ramps—all maintaining a crisp, grid-locked "Solid" appearance.
- **Lighting**: Transitions in the light (shadows on corners) will look "Hand-crafted." Surfaces will catch the sun like chiseled stone, giving the world a more mature, designed aesthetic.

## 4. Grand Scale: "The Horizon is Real"
- **The Feel**: The world will feel **Grand and Boundless**. With the C++ optimizations, we can push the render distance much further. 
- **Exploration**: You can climb a high peak and see far-off biomes and landmarks clearly. Driving vehicles will feel much better because the terrain will load faster than you can travel, meaning you’ll never "outrun" the world.

## 5. Trust: "A World that Remembers"
- **The Feel**: The command-driven system means the terrain is **Trustworthy**. If you build a complex bridge or a deep mine, you know it will stay exactly as you left it. 
- **Changeability**: Because modifications are stored as high-level commands, we could eventually allow "blueprints" or "undo" actions. It turns the terrain into a creative canvas rather than a destructible mask.
